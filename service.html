01 — Product or Service

WHAT DOES CFE SELL?

⚡
Electrical Energy

Generation, transmission and distribution of electricity to homes, industries, commerce and government throughout the national territory. The product is identical for all: standard electricity at 127V/60Hz.

Homogeneous Good
🏠
Target Market

The entire Mexican population. There is no segmentation by choice: any person who needs electricity must contract with CFE. From rural households to large industrial plants.

Captive Market
🎯
Value Proposition

The customer does not choose CFE because it is "better" — they choose it because it is the only option. The value proposition is access to electricity, an essential good with no practical substitute in modern life.

No Competition
